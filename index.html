01 / THE VISION

Local money needs its own infrastructure.

Mobile Money is central to how people pay in many emerging markets, yet moving between local fiat and on-chain digital assets is still too complicated. We're designing a dedicated ledger and node infrastructure that natively embeds these local payment experiences.
